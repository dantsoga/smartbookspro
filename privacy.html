Section 1

Who We Are

SmartBooks Pro, LLC is a professional bookkeeping firm owned and operated by Daniel Tsou, serving small business owners across the United States. We are the data controller for the personal and financial information described in this Policy.

Our services require access to sensitive financial information. We take that responsibility seriously and are committed to handling your data with the care and confidentiality it deserves.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website (smartbooksprous.com), our client questionnaire, and in the course of providing bookkeeping services.

Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and improve our services:

  • Deliver bookkeeping services — reconcile accounts, categorize transactions, prepare financial statements
  • Set up and manage QuickBooks Online — create your chart of accounts, import history, configure reporting
  • Invoice and bill — generate and send invoices for our services
  • Communicate — send reports, respond to questions, request missing documents
  • Recommend services — use your business profile to suggest the right plan
  • Comply with law — meet record-keeping and legal obligations

We do not: sell your information, use it for advertising, share it with unrelated third parties, or use it for any purpose beyond delivering your bookkeeping services.

Section 4

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

QuickBooks Online (Intuit, Inc.)

Your financial records are managed within QuickBooks Online, a platform operated by Intuit, Inc. By using our services, you acknowledge that your data is stored on Intuit's servers and governed by Intuit's Privacy Statement. We encourage you to review it.

Payment Processing

Invoice payments are processed through QuickBooks Payments (Intuit). We do not store your full credit card or bank account numbers. Intuit's payment security standards and privacy practices apply.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, subpoena, court order, or government authority. Where permitted, we will notify you before complying.

Business Transfers

In the unlikely event SmartBooks Pro, LLC is acquired, merged, or sold, client data may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified of any such transfer and given the option to terminate your engagement.

With Your Explicit Consent

We may share your information with your CPA, tax professional, or other advisors — but only with your prior written authorization.

Section 5

Data Storage and Security

Primary storage: Your financial data lives in QuickBooks Online (Intuit's cloud infrastructure), which uses bank-grade encryption and SOC 2 compliant security controls.

Document exchange: Documents shared via email or WhatsApp are stored in password-protected cloud storage accessible only to authorized personnel (Daniel Tsou).

Measures we take:

  • Access to client accounts is restricted to Daniel Tsou only
  • We use strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication on all financial platforms
  • We do not store full account numbers — only the last four digits needed to identify accounts
  • We do not access your accounts for any purpose beyond the agreed services
  • We use encrypted email where available for transmitting sensitive documents

No system is 100% secure. While we take reasonable and industry-standard precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute protection against all security threats. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at contact@smartbooksprous.com.

Section 6

Data Retention

We retain your financial records for a minimum of 7 years following the end of our engagement. This is consistent with IRS guidelines for business record retention and standard accounting practice.

Your contact information (name, email, phone) may be retained for up to 3 years after engagement ends for follow-up communications, unless you request deletion sooner.

Upon your written request, we will:

  • Delete your contact and business profile information from our systems
  • Provide a data export from QuickBooks Online
  • Remove your access credentials from any shared accounts

We may retain financial records as required by law even after a deletion request, but we will inform you of what we are required to keep and why.

Section 8

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website (smartbooksprous.com) is a static informational site. We use minimal web technologies and no advertising or behavioral tracking.

What we do use:

  • Google Fonts — loaded from Google's servers to display our chosen typeface. Google may log your IP address when serving fonts. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • No analytics cookies — we do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tracking services.
  • No advertising cookies — we do not run retargeting or ad campaigns through our website.
  • Session memory — our questionnaire page uses browser session state (not cookies) to remember your answers as you move through the form. This data is not transmitted until you submit.

Because we use no persistent tracking cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and add the required notice.

Section 9

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended solely for business owners and professionals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately at contact@smartbooksprous.com and we will delete it promptly.
