New Client FAQ

Questions We Often Hear

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out any time — we're happy to help.

How long does the setup process take? +
For most clients, QuickBooks setup and initial categorization takes 5–10 business days from when we receive your documents. We'll keep you posted throughout.
Do I need to send documents every month? +
Once you're set up, most clients simply give us read-only access to their bank and credit card feeds through QuickBooks Online, so documents flow automatically. No more monthly uploads.
What if I have questions between monthly reports? +
Reach out any time by email or WhatsApp (Monday–Friday). We're here to support you, not just deliver reports.
Will you file my taxes? +
We handle bookkeeping and financial reporting. For tax filing, we can coordinate directly with your CPA or refer you to a trusted tax professional.
Can I upgrade or change my plan? +
Absolutely. Just send us a message and we'll adjust your plan with 30 days' notice. No penalties, no hassle.
Is my financial information secure? +
Yes. QuickBooks Online uses bank-grade encryption. When sharing documents via email or WhatsApp, avoid sending full account numbers — the last 4 digits are enough for us to identify accounts.
