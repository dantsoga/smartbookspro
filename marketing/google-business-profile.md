# Google Business Profile — Setup Kit

Everything below is ready to paste. Setup takes ~20 minutes at
https://business.google.com (sign in with the Google account you want to manage it from).

## 1. Basic info

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Primary category | **Bookkeeping service** |
| Additional categories | Accountant, Accounting firm |
| Phone | +1 305-495-7636 |
| Website | https://smartbooksprous.com |
| Email | contact@smartbooksprous.com |

**Business type:** choose **Service-area business** (you serve clients at their
location / remotely — do NOT enter your home address as a storefront; Google will
hide the address but you must still verify with it). Set the service area to the
cities/metros you want to rank in (e.g., Miami, Hialeah, Fort Lauderdale, Orlando,
Tampa — up to 20 areas). You can serve all of the US, but list the Florida metros
where Spanish-speaking owners search; that's where the map pack matters.

**Hours:** set real working hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00). Profiles with hours
rank better than "hours unknown."

short `g.page/r/...` URL. Then send it to every happy client:

**EN template:**

> review would help other small business owners find us: {review_link}. Thank you!

**ES template:**

When you have 3+ real Google reviews, we'll re-enable the testimonials section on
the website using them (real, verifiable, FTC-safe).
